Privacy policy

Zoomverse · Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version: Zoomverse has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of mine. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed to handle an optional tip.

What the app stores

Zoomverse remembers a few small settings on your device only: whether you've seen the swipe hint, whether the support suggestion was already shown, and how many tips you've left (to say thank you). They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

Zoomverse doesn't ask for any permission: no camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Tips

You can leave an optional tip from the Support screen. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their own privacy policies. Zoomverse never sees your payment details or your name, and tips don't unlock anything.

To process tips, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your tip history for Zoomverse, and basic technical information such as your device type, system version, app version, country and IP address. This information is not linked to your name or email, and is not used for advertising or tracking. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Zoomverse is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for personal information and does not show ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

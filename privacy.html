Privacy policy

Zoomverse · Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version: Zoomverse collects no data about you. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of mine.

What the app stores

Zoomverse remembers a few small settings on your device only: whether you've seen the swipe hint, whether the support suggestion was already shown, and how many tips you've left (to say thank you). They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

Zoomverse doesn't ask for any permission: no camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Tips

You can leave an optional tip from the Support screen. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their own privacy policies. Zoomverse never sees your payment details or your name, and tips don't unlock anything.

Children

Zoomverse is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for personal information and does not show ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
